BKOS CURRENT AFFAIRS RULES

PURPOSE

Standardize current affairs processing.

RULE 01

Every news item must have source.

RULE 02

Every news item must have date.

RULE 03

Every news item must have category.

RULE 04

Every news item must map to exam relevance.

RULE 05

Every news item must support revision.

RULE 06

Every news item must support version control.
