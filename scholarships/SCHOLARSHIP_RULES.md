BKOS SCHOLARSHIP RULES

PURPOSE

Define the rules governing scholarship discovery,
eligibility, applications, tracking, benefits,
education support, and career mapping.

These rules are mandatory for all scholarship records.

==================================================

RULE 01

UNIQUE IDENTIFIER

Every scholarship must have:

scholarship_id

The identifier must be unique.

Example

SCH-001

SCH-002

==================================================
