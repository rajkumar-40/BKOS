BKOS KNOWLEDGE RULES

PURPOSE

Define the universal rules governing all knowledge,
learning, practice, mastery, skills and career mapping.

No module may violate these rules.

==================================================

KNOWLEDGE FIRST

All educational content must originate from knowledge.

Knowledge is the root object.
